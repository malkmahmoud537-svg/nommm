Pacific Seahorse

Hippocampus ingens • Giant Seahorse

Scientific Classification

Kingdom:Animalia
Phylum:Chordata
Class:Actinopterygii
Order:Syngnathiformes
Family:Syngnathidae
Genus:Hippocampus

One of the largest seahorse species on Earth, possessing an equine head, armored bony plates, a prehensile grasping tail, and male brood pouch gestation.

Habitat
Eelgrass & Mangroves
Diet
Mysid Shrimp & Copepods
Lifespan
3 – 5 Years
Height
Up to 30 cm

Unique Male Pregnancy Biology

Seahorses are celebrated for male pregnancy. During a courtship dance, the female deposits several hundred eggs into the male's specialized abdominal brood pouch. The male fertilizes the eggs internally, providing oxygen, nutrients, and regulating pouch salinity for 14 to 28 days before giving birth to miniature free-swimming fry.

Feeding Mechanism & Threats

Lacking teeth or a stomach, seahorses feed almost continuously by rapidly expanding their tubular snout to create negative pressure, sucking tiny crustaceans out of the water column. Millions of seahorses are harvested annually for traditional medicines, while bottom trawling destroys their critical seagrass root systems.

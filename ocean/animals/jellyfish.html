Cnidarian (Scyphozoa) IUCN: Least Concern

Moon Jellyfish

Aurelia aurita • Saucer Jelly

Scientific Classification

Kingdom:Animalia
Phylum:Cnidaria
Class:Scyphozoa
Order:Semaeostomeae
Family:Ulmaridae
Genus:Aurelia

Ethereal, translucent gelatinous cnidarians consisting of 95% water, drifting effortlessly on oceanic currents and filtering microplankton with radial canals.

Habitat
Coastal Oceans
Diet
Zooplankton & Plankton
Lifespan
6 – 12 Months
Bell Diameter
25 – 40 cm

Anatomy & Four Horseshoe Gonads

Moon jellyfish are easily identified by the four violet or pink horseshoe-shaped gonads visible through their clear saucer-like bell. They have no brain, blood, or heart, coordinating swimming through a decentralized nerve net.

Ecological Role & Threats

They serve as vital food sources for leatherback sea turtles and ocean sunfish. Ocean acidification and warming temperatures often trigger massive jellyfish population blooms due to the overfishing of competing planktivorous fish.

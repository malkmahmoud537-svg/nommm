Giant Manta Ray

Mobula birostris • Oceanic Manta

Scientific Classification

Kingdom:Animalia
Phylum:Chordata
Class:Chondrichthyes
Order:Myliobatiformes
Family:Mobulidae
Genus:Mobula

Majestic, gentle pelagic gliders boasting wingspans up to 7 meters (23 ft) and the highest brain-to-body mass ratio of all cold-blooded fish.

Habitat
Pelagic & Coral Reefs
Diet
Zooplankton Filterer
Lifespan
40 – 50 Years
Wingspan
Up to 7 meters

Cephalic Horns & Somresault Feeding

Giant mantas possess flexible paddle-like cephalic lobes flanking their mouth. When cruising, these lobes roll up into horn-like projections (hence the historic nickname "devilfish"). When encountering rich zooplankton blooms, they unfurl to funnel krill, copepods, and shrimp into their mouth while performing continuous underwater barrel rolls.

Cleaning Stations & Conservation Threats

Mantas regularly visit specific coral pinnacle "cleaning stations" where cleaner wrasses remove parasites and dead skin. Tragically, mantas are targeted for their gill plates (branchial filaments) used in unproven traditional medicinal remedies, and suffer catastrophic mortality from accidental entanglement in drift gillnets.

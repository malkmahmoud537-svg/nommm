Scorpionfish (Scorpaenidae) IUCN: Least Concern

Red Lionfish

Pterois volitans • Turkeyfish

Scientific Classification

Kingdom:Animalia
Phylum:Chordata
Class:Actinopterygii
Order:Scorpaeniformes
Family:Scorpaenidae
Genus:Pterois

Visually mesmerizing predatory fish adorned with 18 venomous defensive spines, celebrated in the Indo-Pacific but a devastating invasive predator in the Atlantic and Caribbean.

Habitat
Coral & Artificial Reefs
Diet
Juvenile Fish & Crustaceans
Lifespan
10 – 15 Years
Spines
18 Venomous Spines

Neurotoxic Spine Weaponry

Lionfish possess 13 dorsal, 2 pelvic, and 3 anal spines containing powerful acetylcholine-based neuromuscular toxins. While rarely fatal to humans, a sting produces excruciating pain, swelling, and temporary paralysis, shielding lionfish from natural predators.

Invasive Crisis in the Caribbean Basin

Accidentally released via the aquarium trade in the late 1980s, lionfish now infest the Western Atlantic from Rhode Island to Brazil. Having no native predators in these waters, a single lionfish can reduce native juvenile reef fish biomass by 79% in just five weeks, threatening entire reef trophic structures.

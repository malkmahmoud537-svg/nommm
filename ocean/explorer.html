Coral Reefs (Rainforests of the Sea)

Depth: 0 – 50 meters Temp: 23°C – 29°C Salinity: 34 – 37 PSU

Teeming with over 25% of all oceanic life despite covering less than 0.1% of the seafloor. Corals build calcium carbonate structures through mutualistic symbiosis with photosynthetic zooxanthellae algae.

Indigenous Fauna: Clownfish, Parrotfish, Hawksbill Sea Turtles, Moray Eels, Reef Sharks, Sea Anemones
Section B • Ecological Crisis

THE CRISIS BENEATH THE SURFACE

Human industrial activity is destabilizing ancient marine equilibriums. Examine the six critical pressures threatening our global waters.

🛍️

Plastic Pollution

Over 14 million tons of plastic enter our oceans annually. Marine mammals and sea turtles mistake floating plastic bags for jellyfish, suffering fatal blockages.

🔬

Microplastics

Synthetic fibers and degraded plastic particles smaller than 5mm now saturate every trophic level of the marine food web, from plankton to apex pelagic fish.

🛢️

Petroleum & Oil Spills

Catastrophic offshore drilling blowouts and tanker ballast washing coat sea bird plumage, destroy sea otter thermal insulation, and poison sensitive larval fish.

🧪

Chemical Waste & Runoff

Agricultural nitrogen fertilizers and industrial effluents create massive hypoxic "dead zones" devoid of oxygen, such as the 15,000 km² dead zone in the Gulf of Mexico.

🎣

Overfishing & Ghost Nets

Over 34% of global fish stocks are overexploited. Commercial trawlers abandon hundreds of thousands of miles of indestructible synthetic gillnets that drift endlessly killing wildlife.

🌡️

Coral Bleaching & Acidification

Excess oceanic carbon absorption lowers seawater pH, dissolving calcium carbonate shells. Marine heatwaves have bleached and killed over 50% of living coral reefs worldwide.
